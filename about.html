About Kunal

Shree Kunal Dhurve

A Vastu consultant practicing since 2012, with a focus on straightforward, practical application of classical principles to modern spaces

Education & Training

Studied under Vastu Master Shri Rameshwar Jyotirmaharishi in Varanasi, with 8 years of field application across 300+ properties

Approach

You receive a structured report with three categories: enhancements (priority order), optional changes, and neutral observations. No "must-do" pressure - just professional recommendations

My process

Four clear steps from first contact to full report

  1. 01

    Understanding

    A short conversation about the property, your goals, and any concerns. We agree on scope before I visit

  2. 02

    Assessment

    On-site evaluation with accurate compass readings, measurements of key zones, and visual analysis

  3. 03

    Analysis

    Contours of property, space usage patterns and orientation checked against classical vastu principles

  4. 04

    Recommendations

    Clear written report with prioritised recommendations - enhancements, optional suggestions and neutral observations
