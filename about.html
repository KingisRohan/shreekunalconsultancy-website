About

Shree Kunal Dhurve

A Vastu consultant practicing since 2012, with a focus on straightforward, practical application of classical principles to modern spaces

Book a Consultation

Kunal Dhurve has been consulting on Vastu Shastra since 2012. Based in Andheri West, he works with homeowners, businesses and industrial operators across Mumbai and, by remote consultation, across India

His practice is grounded in the classical Vastu texts and refined through years of field application across flats, bungalows, offices, showrooms, factories and warehouses. Each engagement is scoped to the property and the client’s goals

Education & Training

Formal study of classical Vastu Shastra, with continued learning under senior practitioners. Applied training through hundreds of property consultations across residential, commercial and industrial contexts

Approach

Every consultation follows a clear four-step method. Clients receive a written report with prioritised recommendations - enhancements, optional changes, and neutral observations. No “must-do” pressure. If a space already works well, that is what the report will say

What clients value

Honest assessment. Clear language. Reports that prioritise what actually matters over what looks impressive. Recommendations that respect the working environment and the budget
