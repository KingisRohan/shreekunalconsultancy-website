गुरु परम्परा

Guruji Shree Kunal Acharya

Vāstu Śāstra came to Guruji as it has come to every practitioner before him — not from a course, but from a house in which it was already being kept.

The practice is generational. It was learned first by watching, then by being corrected, and afterwards through a Guru Paramparā rooted in South Indian Vāstu Vidyā — the line in which the eighty-one padas of the Paramaśāyika maṇḍala, the devatās seated in them, and the order in which doṣas are addressed are held as they were given.

Vāstu is not read from a book at a desk. A drawing shows walls; it does not show which quarter the light enters from, where the ground falls, or what the family has been carrying since they moved. Guruji asks first and measures after.

शिक्षाThe learning

परम्पराAncestral practice, carried within the family and continued under a Guru Paramparā.
विद्याSouth Indian Vāstu Vidyā — the Paramaśāyika maṇḍala of eighty-one padas and the Aṣṭa Dikpāla.

निर्देश

What is given

निर्देशNirdeśa — the guidance, spoken

Guruji gives the correction himself, in his own voice — what stays, what turns, what is placed and in which quarter. Nothing is passed through an assistant and nothing is handed over as a document to be interpreted alone. What is unclear is asked and answered.

यन्त्र स्थापनYantra Sthāpana — the yantra, prepared

A yantra is drawn for that dwelling alone and for the family living in it, consecrated in Guruji's own practice with the saṅkalpa spoken over it, and sent to the house. It is prepared, never sold as an article.
