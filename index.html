वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम्

The whole earth is one dwelling.

अयं निजः परो वेति गणना लघुचेतसाम् ।
उदारचरितानां तु वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम् ॥

“This one is mine, that one a stranger — so reckon the small of heart. For those of noble conduct, the earth itself is the family.” — Mahā Upaniṣad VI.71

Vāstu Śāstra reads a dwelling as a body: its directions, its five elements, its centre. Guruji Shree Kunal Acharya keeps that reading in the manner it was given to him, for houses and places of work wherever they stand.

अष्ट दिक्पाल

The eight quarters, and the centre

Every dwelling is divided into eighty-one padas. Each quarter has a lord seated in it, and each lord governs what may be placed there. This is the grid laid over every plan that is read.

North-East ईशान Īśāna

The seat of Śiva. Water is drawn here, and the household's clarity with it. Kept light, kept open.

North उत्तर Kubera

Kubera holds the treasury. What flows in enters from this quarter.

North-West वायव्य Vāyu

Air, movement, what does not stay. Guests, transit, the restless corner.

East पूर्व Indra

Indra and the first light. The quarter of rule, of beginning, of the opened door.

The Centre ब्रह्मस्थान Brahmasthāna

The nine central padas of the eighty-one. Left unburdened, unbuilt upon, unweighted. The dwelling breathes here or it does not breathe.

West पश्चिम Varuṇa

The waters and the settling of the day. Gain that arrives late, and rest.

South-East आग्नेय Agni

Agni's quarter. Fire is seated here — the hearth, the kitchen, and everything that must be transformed.

South दक्षिण Yama

Yama, who keeps the account. Weight, duty, and what must be borne rather than avoided.

South-West नैऋत्य Nirṛti

The heaviest quarter. Mass is placed here — the earth held down so the rest may rise.

कार्यक्षेत्र

Where the reading is applied

Six kinds of place. The same maṇḍala is laid over each; what differs is which quarter is read most closely.

The dwelling

गृह वास्तुGṛha Vāstu

ईशानĪśāna · the quarter chiefly read

Homes, flats and houses, standing or still being raised.

What is read
  • The true north fixed, and the maṇḍala laid over the plan
  • The Brahmasthāna located and read
  • Each room set against the devatā seated in its quarter
  • The doṣas named, in order of weight

निर्देश

What is given

निर्देशNirdeśa — the guidance, spoken

Guruji gives the correction himself, in his own voice — what stays, what turns, what is placed and in which quarter. Nothing is passed through an assistant and nothing is handed over as a document to be interpreted alone. What is unclear is asked and answered.

यन्त्र स्थापनYantra Sthāpana — the yantra, prepared

A yantra is drawn for that dwelling alone and for the family living in it, consecrated in Guruji's own practice with the saṅkalpa spoken over it, and sent to the house. It is prepared, never sold as an article.
