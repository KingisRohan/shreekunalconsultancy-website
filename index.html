What is Vastu Shastra

An ancient science of orientation, layout, and energy flow - applied to the spaces you live and work in

Vastu Shastra is the classical Indian discipline of architecture and placement. It studies how the orientation of a building, the position of rooms, and the movement of light and air affect the way a space supports the people in it. Kunal applies it to modern flats, offices, shops, factories and plots - without mysticism, with practical recommendations you can act on

My approach

A clear four-step method, not a ritual

You always know what stage you are at, what I am doing, and what you will receive at the end

  1. 01

    Understanding

    A short conversation about the property, your goals, and any concerns. We agree on scope before I visit

  2. 02

    Assessment

    On-site or floor-plan review with accurate compass readings and measurement of the key zones

  3. 03

    Analysis

    Detailed study against classical principles, cross-checked against practical use of the space

  4. 04

    Recommendations

    A prioritised list of changes and remedies, in plain language, with a follow-up included
